# Maria Benkhalti 
Health technology assessment and evidence synthesis methods, now applied to AI. 

I've spent twenty years evaluating health evidence for institutions that decide what to fund — a hospital HTA unit in Quebec, the Public Health Agency of Canada, Health Canada, and the WHO Collaborating Centre for HTA in health equity. I've served as an invited external expert with Canada's Drug Agency (CDA-AMC). 

Today I'm co-founder and CEO of **Palmera Health Sciences**, where we build AI software for HTA submissions and reimbursement dossiers. Day to day, that means working hands-on with large language models on evidence tasks: designing AI-assisted workflows, evaluating model outputs against HTA methods standards (GRADE, risk of bias, systematic review quality), and mapping where current models are reliable and where they fail. 

## What you'll find here 
Most of my code is in private repositories (commercial work). Public repos here are methods projects and evaluation frameworks: 
- **[nma-replication]** — replication of a published network meta-analysis in R (`netmeta`), with notes on ITC feasibility assessment (in progress)
- **[llm-eval-equity-evidence-synthesis]** — evaluation framework (rubrics, prompts, scoring) for testing LLM reliability on considering equity in evidence synthesis tasks (in progress)
